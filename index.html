An old family recipe, kept the old way.

Jange Dai learned kurauni the way it has always been made: with fresh buffalo milk, a heavy iron pot, and time. There are no shortcuts here. The milk is brought to a gentle boil and stirred continuously as it reduces into the rich, caramelized treat we grew up with.

Every batch is made in small quantities, cooled carefully, and sealed fresh for your home. Just milk, craft, and a taste that belongs to Nepal.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

How long does kurauni stay fresh? +

Keep it refrigerated in its sealed tub and enjoy within 10–15 days. Always use a clean, dry spoon.

How much is delivery? +

Delivery inside Ring Road is a flat Rs. 100. For other Kathmandu Valley locations, we'll confirm the delivery charge with you before dispatch.

How should I store and serve it? +

Refrigerate as soon as it arrives. Enjoy it chilled, or let it sit out for a few minutes for a softer, creamier bite.

Can I pay by Fonepay or Cash on Delivery? +

Yes. Choose Fonepay QR or Cash on Delivery in the order form. We'll send your payment details or confirm COD when we message you back.
